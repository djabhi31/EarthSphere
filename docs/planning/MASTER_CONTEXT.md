# EarthPulse AI

## Project Identity

EarthPulse AI is a next-generation Earth Intelligence Platform powered by NASA EONET, NASA GIBS, satellite imagery, geospatial analytics, artificial intelligence, and immersive data visualization.

The platform must feel like a combination of:

* Apple
* Tesla
* Stripe
* Linear
* Arc Browser
* Windy
* FlightRadar24
* Google Earth
* NASA Worldview

The product must not feel like a dashboard.

The product must feel alive.

---

# Product Mission

Transform Earth observation data into beautiful, understandable, actionable intelligence.

Users should instantly understand:

* What is happening?
* Where is it happening?
* Why is it happening?
* How severe is it?
* What may happen next?

---
