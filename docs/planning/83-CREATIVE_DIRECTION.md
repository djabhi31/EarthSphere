# EarthPulse AI — Creative Direction

## Vision

EarthPulse AI is not a dashboard.

It is a living digital Earth experience.

Users should feel like they are exploring the planet instead of using a web application.

---

## Inspiration

Take inspiration from:

- Apple
- Stripe
- Linear
- Arc Browser
- Vercel
- Framer
- Windy
- FlightRadar24
- Google Earth
- NASA Worldview

Never copy designs.

Create an original design language.

---

## Emotional Journey

Every user should experience:

1. Curiosity
2. Discovery
3. Exploration
4. Understanding
5. Confidence
6. Inspiration

---

## Design Personality

The interface should feel:

- Premium
- Scientific
- Elegant
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Futuristic

Avoid generic dashboard aesthetics.

---

## Motion Philosophy

Motion should communicate meaning.

Preferred techniques:

- Spring animations
- Shared layout transitions
- Scroll-linked storytelling
- GPU accelerated transforms

Avoid decorative animations without purpose.

---

## Scroll Storytelling

Scrolling should feel like a moving camera.

Suggested sequence:

Darkness

↓

Earth Appears

↓

Earth Rotates

↓

Wildfires Illuminate

↓

Storms Form

↓

Analytics Grow

↓

Satellite Layers

↓

Future Vision

↓

Call To Action

---

## 3D Philosophy

Earth is the central character.

Future enhancements:

- Atmosphere
- Clouds
- Day/Night cycle
- Satellite orbits
- NASA imagery overlays

---

## Light Mode

Light mode should feel like morning.

Use:

- Warm whites
- Soft blues
- Gentle shadows
- Clean typography

Never simply invert dark mode.

---

## Dark Mode

Dark mode should feel like outer space.

Use:

- Deep blacks
- Space blues
- Ambient glow
- Cinematic lighting

---

## Buttons

Buttons should include:

- Magnetic hover
- Glow
- Spring interaction
- Ripple feedback
- Accessible focus states

---

## Cards

Cards should include:

- Spotlight hover
- Tilt
- Depth
- Dynamic shadows
- Smooth reveal

---

## Maps

Maps are storytelling tools.

Support:

- Smooth fly-to
- Animated markers
- Heatmaps
- Layer transitions
- Timeline playback

---

## Charts

Charts should include:

- Animated entry
- Interactive legends
- Glass tooltips
- Smooth transitions

---

## WOW Moments

Every page should contain:

- 1 Major WOW Moment
- 3 Medium WOW Moments
- Multiple micro-interactions

---

## Accessibility

Support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- Reduced motion
- High contrast
- Visible focus

---

## Performance

Targets:

- Lighthouse >95
- 60 FPS animations
- CLS <0.1
- LCP <2.5 seconds

---

## Final Quality Gate

Before shipping any page ask:

- Does it feel premium?
- Does it feel memorable?
- Does it feel alive?
- Does it match the EarthPulse design language?

If not, continue refining.
