# 68-SCROLL_CAMERA_SYSTEM.md

## Purpose

Scrolling must not simply reveal content.

Scrolling must behave like a camera moving through a cinematic Earth experience.

Users should feel they are travelling through a living digital planet.

---

## Core Principle

Scroll = Camera Movement

Every major section transition must feel like:

- Zoom
- Pan
- Rotate
- Focus
- Reveal

instead of traditional page scrolling.

---

## Camera Timeline

### Scene 1 — Earth Awakening

Viewport: 0% → 15%

- Earth slowly rotates
- Atmosphere glow visible
- Stars move subtly
- Earth scales from 0.8 → 1.0
- Headline fades upward
- Particle field increases density

Goal: Create wonder.

### Scene 2 — Live Earth Intelligence

Viewport: 15% → 35%

- Zoom closer to Earth
- Active event markers illuminate
- Connection lines animate
- Disaster hotspots pulse

Goal: Show Earth is alive.

### Scene 3 — Category Exploration

Viewport: 35% → 55%

- Orbit Earth horizontally
- Focus on Wildfires, Storms, Volcanoes, Floods
- UI colors adapt dynamically

Goal: Tell a story through movement.

### Scene 4 — Timeline Journey

Viewport: 55% → 75%

- Pull away from Earth
- Timeline draws itself
- Earth rotates to event locations

Goal: Connect geography and history.

### Scene 5 — Analytics

Viewport: 75% → 90%

- Charts emerge from Earth
- Data grows organically

Goal: Transform events into intelligence.

### Scene 6 — Final Vision

Viewport: 90% → 100%

- Pull back into space
- Earth glows
- Particles converge
- CTA emerges

Goal: Leave a lasting impression.
