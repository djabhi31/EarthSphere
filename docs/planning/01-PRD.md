# Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

EarthPulse AI

## Product Type

Global Earth Intelligence Platform

## Mission

Provide real-time situational awareness of natural events across the globe through beautiful visualization, advanced analytics, and AI-powered interpretation.

## Target Users

### General Public

Track natural disasters worldwide.

### Researchers

Study environmental patterns.

### Journalists

Monitor developing stories.

### Government Agencies

Disaster response planning.

### NGOs

Humanitarian operations.

### Enterprises

Supply chain and risk monitoring.
